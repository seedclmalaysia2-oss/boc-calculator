Disclaimer

1) This calculator is not intended to determine the final lens specifications, as it does not take into account any topography images reflecting the actual eye condition before or after fitting BOC Ortho-K lenses.

2) This calculator is solely for selecting the initial trial lenses to be fitted on the patient. Further adjustments to the lens parameters will be necessary if the initial trial lens is deemed unsuitable after the fitting process.

3) No data storage, capture, or screenshots are performed for any data entries. No information is saved on any server or location.
